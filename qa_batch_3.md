## Q1: [Labor Law] For Overseas Chinese: How is property divided in cross-border divorce?

**A**: Cross-border divorce property division involves multiple legal systems: 1) Determine jurisdiction court first; 2) Chinese property follows Chinese law, overseas property may follow local law; 3) Different countries have different standards for marital property; 4) Prenuptial agreements recommended.

**法律依据**: 《涉外民事关系法律适用法》

**领域**: 婚姻家事 | **语言**: English

## Q2: [Criminal Defense] What is the statute of limitations for labor arbitration?

**A**: According to Article 27 of the Labor Dispute Mediation and Arbitration Law, the limitation period is one year from when the party knew or should have known their rights were infringed. For wage disputes during ongoing employment, the one-year limit does not apply; but after termination, claims must be filed within one year.

**法律依据**: 《劳动争议调解仲裁法》

**领域**: 劳动法 | **语言**: English
